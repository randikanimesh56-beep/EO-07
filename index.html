Strategy rules and important limitations
Trend continuation: completed 5M EMA20/50 direction + ADX14 at or above 20. On 1M, a recent pullback must come near EMA20; the latest completed candle must close in the trend direction, break the previous candle high/low, have body/range at least 50%, and not be more than 0.8 ATR from EMA20.

Liquidity reversal: used only when the 5M ADX is below 20. A completed candle must sweep beyond the previous 10-candle high/low and close back inside; the following completed candle must confirm a short-term structure break with displacement.

Risk filters: M1 ATR14 must be between 0.6x and 2.0x its recent 20-ATR median; a signal candle larger than 2.0 ATR is rejected. Live data must be fresh. If the optional Twelve Data feed is connected, it must be fresh and within the configured price-difference limit. Loaded high/medium-impact news can block signals.

These thresholds are explicit initial test settings, not parameters proven optimal. The 5M candles are aggregated only from five consecutive completed 1M candles. No order is sent to any broker.
